What we do

About KU ACM

We are the largest Engineering student organization at the University of Kansas. We are a student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).

Weekly Meetings

KU ACM hosts weekly meetings that provide presentations from local companies and student presentations showing off cutting edge technologies. Don't forget to grab a slice of pizza while you watch!

BugSmashers

bugSmashers is ACM's Programming Club, a group of students who meet weekly to discuss programming topics and learn new skills. Click the bugSmashers tab to learn more!

HackKU

HackKU is an annual 36-hour hackathon at the University of Kansas where students have the opportunity to create new ideas, discover different paths, and push the boundaries of technology. Visit the HackKU tab to learn more!

Tutoring

Tutoring KU ACM also provides free tutoring for many engineering classes including most EECS classes 500 level and below, Calculus 1-3, and linear algebra.
